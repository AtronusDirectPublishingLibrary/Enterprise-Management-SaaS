1. INTRODUCTION

Ethio-BioPharma Enterprise (EBPE) stands at a pivotal moment, with a visionary mandate to
establish a fully integrated, end-to-end pharmaceutical and vaccine manufacturing campus in
Ethiopia. This strategic roadmap (2026 - 2035) charts the course for transforming that vision into
a self-sustaining commercial and public health reality. It outlines a phased, financially disciplined,
talent-centered, and digitally enabled transformation plan.

To implement this complex mission, EBPE will adopt an integrated yet staggered approach in all
its Business Units which will begin in Q3 2026. The "quick-win" units (Diagnostics, PPE and medical
supplies, and Medicines and Input Material) are designed for rapid market entry (18-month
completion targets) to generate early cash flow, which will support the longer-term, capital-
intensive vaccine and API manufacturing unit. This integrated start allows for shared
infrastructure, centralized procurement, and a unified corporate launch.

A Hybrid Cross-Subsidization Financial Model will be followed. Early revenues from Diagnostics,
Medical Supplies and PPE, and Medicines (Generics) will contribute to the ShieldVax unit's CAPEX.
Crucially, a Bridge Financing Facility will be secured for Phase 2 (2028-2029) to safeguard the
ShieldVax launch against early market volatility. By 2035, EBPE aims to achieve vaccine self-
reliance, regional export competitiveness, and vertical integration in pharmaceutical inputs.

3. HUMAN CAPITAL STRATEGY (CENTER OF THE ROADMAP)

Human resources will be the central pillar of EBPE's growth and long-term sustainability. The
Enterprise will place talent at the core of its strategic agenda by systematically attracting,
developing, and retaining high-caliber professionals across technical, managerial, and leadership
functions. Through structured training programs, succession planning, performance-based
incentives, and institutional culture building, EBPE will ensure continuity, operational excellence,
and sustained competitiveness.

3.1 Workforce Development

· Establish EBPE workforce development program (GMP, QA/QC, validation, regulatory
    science)

· Leadership and succession planning framework
· Diaspora talent engagement initiative
· Postgraduate scholarships in pharmaceutical sciences
· Continuous GMP and digital skills training

3.2 Talent Retention & Culture

· Performance-based incentives
· Clear career progression pathways
· Gender inclusion and youth employment strategy
· International exposure and technical partnerships

7. CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS FOR SIMULTANEOUS START

1. Master Project Management: Establish a single project Implementation team within EBPE to
oversee all parallel construction and commissioning streams.

                                                                                         5
2. Unified Supply Chain: Centralize procurement for raw materials (vials, chemicals, etc.,) to
gain bulk purchasing power.

3. Talent First Institution Culture: Launch the Talent program in partnership with local
Universities, Research Institutes and external partners to train technicians and chemists in GMP,
ensuring a ready workforce for 2027-2028.

4. Regulatory Fast-Track: Work with EFDA to create a "priority path" for multi-product facility
approvals based on international standards.

    · Step 1 (2028): Secure EFDA approval for domestic sales.
    · Step 2 (2030): Target WHO PQ for medicines, diagnostics, PPE and medical supplies

         after 2 years of stable GMP operations.

5. Strong government and industrial policy alignment: Maintain close coordination with relevant
ministries and national strategies to secure policy support, procurement alignment, and
implementation facilitation.

6. Digital transformation embedded from inception: Embed integrated digital systems and AI-
enabled tools across construction, manufacturing, quality, and supply chain functions to enhance
efficiency, compliance, and scalability.
